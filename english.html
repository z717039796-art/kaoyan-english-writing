MODULE 01 · DYNAMIC

动态图表写作全套句式

适用：折线图、动态柱状图（数据随时间变化）。从开篇到结尾，每一句都有模板 + 真题变体。

一、动态图表写作全套句式

适用：折线图、动态柱状图（数据随时间变化）

1. 图表开篇总起句

通用基础模板句

①As is vividly illustrated in the line/bar chart, changes in the three categories concerning 题目相关词 were divided between two upward movements and one downward movement.
翻译：折线/柱状图清晰展示，与题目相关词相关的三类数据变化呈现两类上涨、一类下降的趋势。
②As is vividly illustrated in the line/bar chart, changes in the three categories concerning 题目相关词 fall into three groups: upward movements, downward movements and stable levels. 出现不变数据时用
翻译：折线/柱状图清晰展示，与题目相关词相关的三类变化分为三种趋势：上升、下降、保持平稳。

真题范文改写变体

①As is vividly illustrated in the bar chart, changes in the two categories concerning China's urban and rural populations from 1990 to 2010 were divided between one upward movement and one downward movement. 2014城乡人口
翻译：柱状图清晰展现1990至2010年中国城乡人口两类数据一升一降。
②As is vividly illustrated in the bar chart, changes in the three categories concerning the market share of car brands in the domestic market fall into three groups: upward movements, downward movements and stable levels. 2011汽车品牌
翻译：柱状图中国内汽车品牌市场份额三类数据分为上涨、下跌、平稳三类走势。
③As is vividly illustrated in the bar chart, changes in the two categories concerning mobile-phone subscriptions were divided between two upward movements. 2010手机用户
翻译：柱状图里两类手机用户数据均呈上升趋势。

2. 数据变化描述句

（1）描述上升

①From 时间1 to 时间2, 事物1 rose from 数字1 to 数字2, showing a steady upward trend / a sharp increase.
翻译：从时间1到时间2，事物1数值从数字1增长至数字2，呈现稳步上升/大幅激增。
②From 2008 to 2009, Chinese brands jumped from 25% to 33%, showing a sharp increase. 2011 jump替换rose
翻译：2008到2009年，国产品牌占比从25%猛增至33%，涨幅显著。
③From 1990 to 2010, the urban population rose from 300 million to 670 million, showing a sharp increase. 2014
翻译：1990至2010年，城镇人口从3亿增至6.7亿，大幅上涨。
④From 2000 to 2008, mobile-phone subscriptions in developing countries rose from less than 1 billion to 4 billion, showing a sharp increase. 2010
翻译：2000至2008年，发展中国家手机用户从不足10亿增长至40亿，大幅上升。
⑤Meanwhile, those in developed countries rose from 5 million to 1 billion, showing a steady upward trend. 2010两类上升区分
翻译：与此同时，发达国家手机用户从500万增长至10亿，稳步上涨。

（2）描述下降

①Meanwhile, 事物2 fell from 数字3 to 数字4, indicating a clear decline / a sharp decrease.
翻译：与此同时，事物2数值从数字3降至数字4，出现明显下滑/大幅减少。
②Over the same span, the rural population fell from 830 million to 670 million, indicating a sharp decrease. 2014 over the same span替换meanwhile
翻译：同一时期，农村人口从8.3亿降至6.7亿，大幅减少。
③Meanwhile, Japanese brands fell from 35% to 25%, indicating a sharp decrease. 2011
翻译：同时日系汽车品牌占比从35%下跌至25%，降幅明显。

（3）描述数据持平

By contrast, 事物3 remained stable at roughly 数字5 with little fluctuation.
翻译：与之相反，事物3数据稳定维持在数字5左右，波动极小。
例句：By contrast, American brands remained stable at roughly 15% with little fluctuation. 2011
翻译：反观美系品牌占比稳定在15%上下，几乎无波动。

3. 原因分析段成套句式

句1：引出社会现象

基础模板：The public's demand for 题目相关词 is by no means a recent phenomenon; it has become increasingly evident today because the conditions necessary for satisfying that demand are now more fully in place.
翻译：大众对题目相关词的需求并非新近出现的现象，如今该趋势愈发明显，满足该需求的各类条件已日趋完善。
①The public's desire for urban life is by no means a recent phenomenon; it has become increasingly evident today because the conditions necessary for satisfying that demand are now more fully in place. 2014 desire替换demand
翻译：民众对城市生活的向往并非近期才出现，如今愈发突出，实现定居城市的各类条件已更加完善。
②The public's demand for Chinese car brands is by no means a recent phenomenon; it has become increasingly evident today because the conditions necessary for satisfying that demand are now more fully in place. 2011
翻译：民众青睐国产汽车并非新鲜趋势，如今愈发明显，选购国产车的客观条件已十分完备。
③The public's demand for mobile-phone subscriptions is by no means a recent phenomenon; it has become increasingly evident today because the conditions necessary for satisfying that demand are now more fully in place. 2010
翻译：大众对手机使用的需求早已存在，如今普及趋势愈发显著，使用手机的配套条件已全面完善。

句2：经济/客观基础句

基础模板：Rapid national economic growth has laid a solid material foundation for 题目相关词 to become integrated into everyday life and gain widespread popularity.
翻译：国家经济快速发展，为题目相关词融入日常生活、广泛普及打下坚实物质基础。
①The rapid growth of the Chinese economy has laid a solid material foundation for the rural population to become integrated into urban life and gain widespread popularity. 2014
翻译：中国经济飞速发展，为农村人口融入城市生活奠定坚实物质基础。
②Rapid growth of the Chinese economy has laid a solid material foundation for Chinese car brands to become integrated into everyday life and gain widespread popularity. 2011
翻译：中国经济高速发展，助力国产汽车走进千家万户、普及开来。
③Rapid economic growth in developing countries has laid a solid material foundation for mobile-phone subscriptions to become integrated into everyday life and gain widespread popularity. 2010
翻译：发展中国家经济快速增长，为手机普及奠定物质基础。

句3：社会发展+民众观念转变句

基础模板：Meanwhile, as society continues to advance and individuals become more financially secure, public attitudes evolve accordingly, making people more receptive to 题目相关词.
翻译：与此同时，随着社会持续发展、居民经济条件宽裕，大众观念随之转变，更愿意接纳题目相关词。
①Meanwhile, as urban infrastructure continues to improve and individuals become more financially secure, public attitudes evolve accordingly, making people more receptive to urban living. 2014新增城市基建
翻译：同时城市基础设施持续完善、居民收入提升，民众更向往城市生活。
②Meanwhile, as society continues to advance and individuals become more financially secure, consumers tend to choose cost-effective domestic cars with higher performance at lower prices, making people more receptive to Chinese car brands. 2011补充产品优势
翻译：社会进步、收入提高后，消费者偏爱性价比高、质优价廉的国产车，更认可国产品牌。
③Meanwhile, as society continues to advance and individuals become more financially secure, public attitudes evolve accordingly, making people more receptive to mobile-phone subscriptions. 2010原版套用
翻译：社会不断发展、居民经济宽裕，大众观念改变，普遍接受手机。

句4：双重归因总结句

基础模板：It is therefore evident that the growing prevalence of 题目相关词 owes as much to rising public demand as it does to the practical support that society provides for individual choice.
翻译：由此显而易见，题目相关词日渐普及，既源于民众日益增长的需求，也离不开社会为个人选择提供的配套支撑。
①It is therefore evident that the growing prevalence of urban living owes as much to rising public demand as it does to the practical support that society provides for individual choice. 2014
翻译：可见城市生活普及，一方面源于民众向往，另一方面得益于社会配套保障。
②It is therefore evident that the growing prevalence of Chinese car brands owes as much to the booming Chinese economy as it does to the practical advantage of domestic cars' high performance at lower prices. 2011替换后半段原因
翻译：国产汽车走红，既得益于蓬勃发展的国内经济，也归功于国产车物美价廉的优势。

句5：校园类特殊句式（适用于校园/个人成长话题）

基础模板句1：The deeper value of 题目相关词 lies in enabling individuals to develop a genuine capacity for choice.
翻译：题目相关词更深层的价值在于使个体发展出真正的选择能力。
基础模板句2：Confronted with a multitude of possibilities, those who possess 题目相关词 do not allow external voices to dictate their decisions; rather, they can discern what deserves their commitment and are prepared to bear the costs their choices entail.
翻译：面对众多可能性，拥有题目相关词的人不会让外界声音左右自己的决定；相反，他们能辨别什么值得投入，并准备好承担选择带来的代价。
基础模板句3：Moreover, as 题目相关词 takes deeper root, individuals become more adept at navigating difficulties with composure and flexibility.
翻译：此外，随着题目相关词深入人心，个体变得更善于以从容和灵活的态度应对困难。
基础模板句4：Thus, the most important transformation brought about by 题目相关词 is that it enables individuals to progress from merely having options to possessing the judgment and resolve required to choose wisely.
翻译：因此，题目相关词带来的最重要转变是，它使个体从仅仅拥有选择，进步到拥有明智选择所需的判断力和决心。
①The deeper value of students' part-time jobs lies in enabling individuals to develop a genuine capacity for choice. 2013
翻译：学生兼职更深层的价值在于使个体发展出真正的选择能力。
②Confronted with a multitude of possibilities, those who possess part-time work experience do not allow external voices to dictate their decisions; rather, they discern what deserves their commitment and are prepared to bear the costs their choices entail. 2013
翻译：面对众多可能性，有兼职经历的人不会让外界声音左右自己的决定；相反，他们能辨别什么值得投入，并准备好承担选择带来的代价。
③Meanwhile, a rich spare-time life provides favorable conditions for students' part-time jobs to become integrated into university life and gain widespread popularity. 2013
翻译：同时，丰富的课余生活为学生兼职融入大学生活并广泛普及提供了有利条件。

4. 结尾总结与升华句式

句1：发展总起句

基础模板：Sustaining the development of 题目相关词 depends not merely on how much support is provided, but more importantly on whether such support produces tangible results.

①Sustaining the growth of the urban population depends not merely on how much support is provided, but more importantly on whether such support produces tangible results. 2014 growth替换development
翻译：促进城镇人口持续增长，关键在于配套政策能否落地见效。

翻译：推动国产汽车持续发展，不能只看扶持力度，更要看政策能否带来实际效果。

翻译：手机普及的持续推进，重点在于配套举措能否产生实际成效。

句2：政府对策句（2014独有）

Relevant authorities should both create the necessary conditions and adjust specific measures promptly in light of how the urban population grows in practice.
翻译：有关部门既要完善配套条件，也要结合城镇人口发展现状及时调整政策。

MODULE 02 · STATIC

静态图表写作全套句式

适用：饼图、静态柱状图（占比/排名对比）。数据不随时间变化，重在对比与归因。

二、静态图表写作全套句式

适用：饼图、静态柱状图（数据占比对比，无时间变化）

1. 图表开篇总起句

通用基础模板

Comparison of the five categories associated with 题目相关词 in the bar/pie chart reveals a pronounced ranking pattern.
翻译：柱状/饼图对比五类和题目相关词相关的内容，数据呈现鲜明的层级占比规律。

范文改写变体（2026儿童户外活动）

The comparison of the four categories associated with the survey on children's outdoor activities in the bar chart reveals a pronounced ranking pattern.
翻译：柱状图展示一项关于儿童户外活动的四类调查，各类占比层次分明。
2013范文：The comparison of the four categories related to university students' part-time job participation in the bar chart reveals a pronounced ranking pattern.
翻译：柱状图展示大学生兼职参与度的四类对比，各类占比层次分明。

2. 占比分层描写句式

①With a share of 百分比1, 事物1 ranks well ahead of all the others.
翻译：事物1占比百分比1，遥遥领先其余所有类别。
例句：With a share of 54.6%, satisfying their curiosity ranks well ahead of all the others.
翻译：满足好奇心占比54.6%，位居首位。
2013范文：With a share of 88.24%, seniors rank well ahead of all the others.
翻译：大四学生占比88.24%，遥遥领先其余所有类别。
②事物2 and 事物3 occupy the two middle positions, registering 百分比2 and 百分比3, respectively.
翻译：事物2、事物3处于中间梯队，占比分别为百分比2、百分比3。
例句：Enhancing their powers of observation, building up their bodies and strengthening parent-child interaction, account for 54.5%, 37.2% and 33.2% respectively.
翻译：提升观察力、强身健体、增进亲子互动占比分别为54.5%、37.2%、33.2%。
2013范文：Sophomores and juniors occupy the two middle positions, registering 71.13% and 71.93% respectively.
翻译：大二、大三学生处于中间梯队，占比分别为71.13%、71.93%。
③At the other end of the scale, 事物4 and 事物5 account for the smallest proportions, at only 百分比4 and 百分比5.
翻译：占比垫底的是事物4与事物5，仅分别占百分比4、百分比5。
2013范文：For freshmen, the proportion is only 67.77%.
翻译：大一学生的占比仅为67.77%。

翻译：学生兼职更深层的价值在于使个体发展出真正的选择能力。
②Confronted with a multitude of possibilities, those who possess part-time work experience do not allow external voices to dictate their decisions; rather, they discern what deserves their commitment and are prepared to bear the costs their choices entail. 2013
翻译：面对众多可能性，有兼职经历的人不会让外界声音左右自己的决定；相反，他们能辨别什么值得投入，并准备好承担选择带来的代价。
③Meanwhile, a rich spare-time life provides favorable conditions for students' part-time jobs to become integrated into university life and gain widespread popularity. 2013
翻译：同时，丰富的课余生活为学生兼职融入大学生活并广泛普及提供了有利条件。

动态图范文

范文1：2014年大作文（城乡人口变化）

As is vividly illustrated in the bar chart, changes in the two categories concerning China's urban and rural populations from 1990 to 2010 were divided between one upward movement and one downward movement. From 1990 to 2010, the urban population rose from 300 million to 670 million, showing a sharp increase. Over the same span, the rural population fell from 830 million to 670 million, indicating a sharp decrease.
The public's desire for urban life is by no means a recent phenomenon; it has become increasingly evident today because the conditions necessary for satisfying that demand are now more fully in place. The rapid growth of the Chinese economy has laid a solid material foundation for the rural population to become integrated into urban life and gain widespread popularity. Meanwhile, as urban infrastructure continues to improve and individuals become more financially secure, public attitudes evolve accordingly, making people more receptive to urban living. It is therefore evident that the growing prevalence of urban living owes as much to rising public demand as it does to the practical support that society provides for individual choice.
Sustaining the growth of the urban population depends not merely on how much support is provided, but more importantly on whether such support produces tangible results. Relevant authorities should both create the necessary conditions and adjust specific measures promptly in light of how the urban population grows in practice. Only through effective interaction among all parties can the rural population become genuinely integrated into urban life.
全文翻译：柱状图清晰展现了1990至2010年中国城乡人口两类数据一升一降的变化。1990至2010年，城镇人口从3亿增至6.7亿，大幅上涨。同一时期，农村人口从8.3亿降至6.7亿，大幅减少。民众对城市生活的向往并非近期才出现，如今愈发突出，实现定居城市的各类条件已更加完善。中国经济飞速发展，为农村人口融入城市生活奠定了坚实物质基础。同时，城市基础设施持续完善、居民收入提升，民众观念随之转变，更向往城市生活。由此可见，城市生活日渐普及，一方面源于民众向往，另一方面得益于社会配套保障。促进城镇人口持续增长，关键在于配套政策能否落地见效。有关部门既要完善配套条件，也要结合城镇人口发展现状及时调整政策。只有多方协同配合、良性互动，农村人口才能真正融入城市生活。

范文2：2011年英语二大作文（汽车品牌市场份额）

As is vividly illustrated in the bar chart, changes in the three categories concerning the market share of car brands in the domestic market fall into three groups: upward movements, downward movements and stable levels. From 2008 to 2009, Chinese brands jumped from 25% to 33%, showing a sharp increase. Meanwhile, Japanese brands fell from 35% to 25%, indicating a sharp decrease. By contrast, American brands remained stable at roughly 15% with little fluctuation.
The public's demand for Chinese car brands is by no means a recent phenomenon; it has become increasingly evident today because the conditions necessary for satisfying that demand are now more fully in place. Rapid growth of the Chinese economy has laid a solid material foundation for Chinese car brands to become integrated into everyday life and gain widespread popularity. Meanwhile, as society continues to advance and individuals become more financially secure, consumers tend to choose cost-effective domestic cars with higher performance at lower prices, making people more receptive to Chinese car brands. It is therefore evident that the growing prevalence of Chinese car brands owes as much to the booming Chinese economy as it does to the practical advantage of domestic cars' high performance at lower prices.
Sustaining the development of Chinese car brands depends not merely on how much support is provided, but more importantly on whether such support produces tangible results.
全文翻译：柱状图中国内汽车品牌市场份额三类数据分为上涨、下跌、平稳三类走势。2008到2009年，国产品牌占比从25%猛增至33%，涨幅显著。同时日系品牌占比从35%下跌至25%，降幅明显。反观美系品牌占比稳定在15%上下，几乎无波动。民众青睐国产汽车并非新鲜趋势，如今愈发明显，选购国产车的客观条件已十分完备。中国经济高速发展，助力国产汽车走进千家万户、普及开来。社会进步、收入提高后，消费者偏爱性价比高、质优价廉的国产车，更认可国产品牌。由此可见，国产汽车走红，既得益于蓬勃发展的国内经济，也归功于国产车物美价廉的优势。推动国产汽车持续发展，不能只看扶持力度，更要看政策能否带来实际效果。

范文3：套用2010年大作文（手机用户发展）

As is vividly illustrated in the bar chart, changes in the two categories concerning mobile-phone subscriptions were divided between two upward movements. From 2000 to 2008, mobile-phone subscriptions in developing countries rose from less than 1 billion to 4 billion, showing a sharp increase. Meanwhile, those in developed countries rose from 5 million to 1 billion, showing a steady upward trend.
The public's demand for mobile-phone subscriptions is by no means a recent phenomenon; it has become increasingly evident today because the conditions necessary for satisfying that demand are now more fully in place. Rapid economic growth in developing countries has laid a solid material foundation for mobile-phone subscriptions to become integrated into everyday life and gain widespread popularity. Meanwhile, as society continues to advance and individuals become more financially secure, public attitudes evolve accordingly, making people more receptive to mobile-phone subscriptions.
Sustaining the development of mobile-phone subscriptions depends not merely on how much support is provided, but more importantly on whether such support produces tangible results.
全文翻译：柱状图里两类手机用户数据均呈上升趋势。2000至2008年，发展中国家手机用户从不足10亿增长至40亿，大幅上升。与此同时，发达国家手机用户从500万增长至10亿，稳步上涨。大众对手机使用的需求早已存在，如今普及趋势愈发显著，使用手机的配套条件已全面完善。发展中国家经济快速增长，为手机普及奠定物质基础。社会不断发展、居民经济宽裕，大众观念改变，普遍接受手机。手机普及的持续推进，重点在于配套举措能否产生实际成效。

范文4：2026年英语二大作文（儿童户外活动）

The comparison of the four categories associated with the survey on children's outdoor activities in the bar chart reveals a pronounced ranking pattern. Parents' focus on children's growth has laid a solid material foundation for outdoor activities to become integrated into everyday life and gain widespread popularity. Meanwhile, as society continues to advance and individuals become more financially secure, parental attitudes evolve accordingly, making parents more receptive to children's outdoor activities. It is therefore evident that the growing prevalence of children's outdoor activities owes as much to children's rising demand as it does to the practical support that parents provide for children's choices.

Parents should both create the necessary conditions and adjust specific measures promptly in light of how children develop in practice. Only through effective interaction among all parties can children's outdoor activities become genuinely integrated into everyday life.
全文翻译：柱状图展示一项关于儿童户外活动的四类调查，各类占比层次分明。满足好奇心占比54.6%，位居首位。提升观察力、强身健体、增进亲子互动占比分别为54.5%、37.2%、33.2%。家长重视孩子户外活动并非新鲜趋势，如今该趋势愈发明显，开展户外活动的各类条件已更加完善。家长对孩子成长的重视，为户外活动融入孩子日常生活、普及开来打下基础。社会不断发展、家庭经济条件变好，家长观念随之转变，更加支持孩子参与户外活动。由此可见儿童户外活动普及，一方面源于孩子自身需求，另一方面离不开家长对孩子选择的支持。持续推广儿童户外活动，不只依靠家长支持，更关键在于这份支持能否真正有益于孩子成长。家长应当为孩子创造有利条件，并结合孩子实际成长情况及时调整安排。只有家庭、社会多方协同配合，户外活动才能真正融入孩子的日常生活。

范文5：2013年英语二大作文（大学生兼职）

The comparison of the four categories related to university students' part-time job participation in the bar chart reveals a pronounced ranking pattern. With a share of 88.24%, seniors rank well ahead of all the others. Sophomores and juniors occupy the two middle positions, registering 71.13% and 71.93% respectively. For freshmen, the proportion is only 67.77%.

Meanwhile, a rich spare-time life provides favorable conditions for students' part-time jobs to become integrated into university life and gain widespread popularity.
Sustaining the development of students' part-time jobs depends not merely on how much support is provided, but more importantly on whether such support produces tangible results. Universities should both create the necessary conditions and adjust specific measures promptly in light of how students' part-time jobs develop in practice. Students, for their part, should carefully assess the value of part-time jobs in accordance with their own circumstances.
全文翻译：柱状图展示大学生兼职参与比例的四类对比，数据呈现鲜明的层级规律。大四学生占比88.24%，远超其他年级。大二和大三居中，分别为71.13%和71.93%。大一学生仅占67.77%。学生兼职的深层价值在于培养真正的选择能力。面对众多可能性，有兼职经历的学生不会让外界声音左右自己的决定，而是能辨别什么值得投入，并准备承担选择的代价。同时，丰富的课余生活为学生兼职融入大学生活、广泛普及提供了有利条件。推动学生兼职持续发展，不仅要看支持力度，更要看支持是否带来实际效果。高校应创造必要条件，并根据学生兼职的实际发展情况及时调整具体措施。学生自身也应根据个人情况认真评估兼职的价值。
